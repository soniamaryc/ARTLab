Outreach & Mentoring

Inspiring future innovators through STEM outreach, mentorship, and community engagement. At ART Lab, we believe knowledge should empower and uplift everyone.

Our Commitment to STEM Outreach

The ART Lab is dedicated to advancing STEM education by connecting technology and community. Through hands-on workshops, mentoring initiatives, and public events, we help students discover the excitement of robotics, AR/VR, and innovation. Our outreach mission is to make cutting-edge technology approachable and inspire diverse participation in engineering and science.

Featured Outreach Highlights

STEM School Talks

Engaging K–12 students with interactive robotics demonstrations and storytelling to spark curiosity in engineering and design.

Community Workshops

Hands-on sessions that introduce participants to coding, robotics, and XR tools — encouraging creative problem-solving and collaboration.

Mentorship Programs

Guiding students in research and project development to cultivate leadership and innovation in real-world technology contexts.
