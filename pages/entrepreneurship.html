Entrepreneurship & Innovation

At ART Lab, we believe that innovation begins with curiosity and grows through collaboration. Our entrepreneurial journey bridges research with real-world application — turning robotics prototypes into patented technologies, startups, and industry collaborations that make an impact.

Our Innovation Journey

The ART Lab’s innovation story began with passion-driven research in robotics automation and extended into industrial transformation. Through ventures like the PaintOz Coating Robot, our students and faculty explore how academic innovation becomes a foundation for entrepreneurship. We work closely with global partners like SICK Sensors and Maxbyte Technologies to co-create intelligent, sustainable, and scalable robotic systems.

Beyond products, we aim to foster a culture of innovation — mentoring student founders, supporting patent development, and encouraging cross-disciplinary collaboration for social and technological progress.

Featured Venture

PaintOz Coating Robot

PaintOz is an intelligent surface coating robot that brings automation, safety, and precision to industrial painting. Equipped with advanced computer vision and adaptive motion control, it showcases how innovation in the lab can evolve into market-ready robotics solutions. The project exemplifies ART Lab’s entrepreneurial approach to applied research.
